    The method
    1. Before the run, the site creates a random commitment code and timestamp.
    2. The exact two 6K times are recorded.
    3. At the bar, four taps capture when each beer lands, followed by the printed receipt reference and total.
    4. Those fixed inputs and the ten names are normalized into one record and hashed with SHA-256.
    5. The digest drives an unbiased deterministic Fisher–Yates shuffle. The same evidence always reproduces the same order.

    No chugging component. Drink at your own pace; non-alcoholic beer works identically.
